Keep a password as a salted digest with `password: { securePassword: true }` in `static attributes`, and give each new record a random token with `authToken: { secureToken: true }` (#355). A secure password adds `password`, `passwordConfirmation`, and `passwordChallenge`, is hashed into `passwordDigest` with PBKDF2-SHA256 when the record is saved, and is left out of `toJSON()` with its digest. `await user.authenticate(password)` checks it, and `await User.authenticateBy({ email, password })` finds a record and checks its password in one step. Its validations require it, check its confirmation and challenge, and allow at most 72 bytes. `{ securePassword: { algorithm } }` plugs in another hash, such as bcrypt. A secure token is 24 characters of base58, made as the record is built, or with `on: "create"` as it's created, and `regenerateAuthToken()` saves a new one. Secure passwords, their digests, and secure tokens are always filtered from logs and errors, and left out of `toJSON()`. `generateSecureToken(length)` makes a token for anything else.
